# Existing Crown Movers Website Photo Instructions

Claude may inspect and use any photograph currently published on:

**https://www.crownmovers.ca/**

This permission applies to design-system exploration, page concepts and mockups for the Crown Movers redesign.

## Selection priority

Choose photographs in this order:

1. Branded Crown Movers trucks
2. Uniformed Crown Movers crews at work
3. Furniture wrapped in moving blankets or protective material
4. Residential moving scenes
5. Commercial and office moving scenes
6. Packing, assembly and specialty-item handling
7. Real Montreal buildings, streets, stairs, elevators and loading conditions
8. Team portraits and customer interactions

## Usage rules

- Real company photography should be used before generic stock or AI-generated imagery.
- Images may be cropped, reframed and colour-corrected conservatively.
- Preserve recognizable people, uniforms, truck markings and logos accurately.
- Do not invent testimonials, partnerships, customer identities or scenarios.
- Do not use outdated cartoon service illustrations as the main visual direction.
- Mark low-resolution images as placeholders requiring the original high-resolution source.
- Production assets must be imported into the new WordPress media library rather than hotlinked.
