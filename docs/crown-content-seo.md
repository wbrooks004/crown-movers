# Crown Movers — Content, SEO & Migration Standards

## Purpose

This file defines Crown Movers content standards, multilingual content principles, SEO preservation rules, and migration requirements.

Use it when:

* Writing copy
* Restructuring pages
* Creating service pages
* Creating location pages
* Changing URLs
* Planning navigation
* Planning internal links
* Migrating existing content
* Working with English/French pages

---

## Content Objective

Content should make Crown Movers feel:

* Helpful
* Professional
* Friendly
* Competent
* Trustworthy
* Straightforward
* Reassuring
* Customer-focused

Write like an experienced service company that understands moving customers.

Do not sound like generic AI marketing copy.

---

## Tone

Favor:

* Clear language
* Natural sentences
* Useful explanations
* Specific information
* Calm confidence
* Customer-focused messaging
* Friendly professionalism

Avoid:

* Corporate jargon
* Excessive hype
* Empty superlatives
* Artificial enthusiasm
* Fear-based selling
* Repetitive SEO phrases
* Keyword stuffing
* Generic statements that could describe any mover

Avoid repeatedly describing Crown Movers as:

* Premium
* Luxury
* Best
* Number one
* Unmatched
* World-class

Demonstrate quality rather than constantly claiming it.

---

## Value Communication

The website should communicate that Crown Movers provides strong service and professional execution at an accessible price.

Do not turn affordability into discount positioning.

Prefer messages that communicate:

* Value
* Reliability
* Care
* Organization
* Communication
* Convenience
* Professional standards

over constant emphasis on “cheap” pricing.

---

## Existing Content

The current Crown Movers website may be used as source material.

Existing content may be:

* Rewritten
* Improved
* Consolidated
* Expanded
* Reorganized

Preserve useful information and established search intent.

Do not preserve poor writing merely because it already exists.

Do not remove useful SEO content merely because shorter copy looks visually cleaner.

Balance design and search intent.

---

## Factual Accuracy

Never invent Crown Movers facts.

Do not fabricate:

* Prices
* Guarantees
* Credentials
* Statistics
* Review counts
* Awards
* Service areas
* Insurance coverage
* Fleet details
* Team size
* Years of experience
* Customer numbers
* Special certifications

If a useful claim cannot be verified, omit it or mark it as needing confirmation.

---

## URL Preservation

Preserve existing URLs whenever reasonably possible.

A cleaner-looking URL alone is not sufficient reason to change an established URL.

Before changing an existing URL, consider:

* Existing rankings
* Search intent
* Backlinks
* Indexed history
* Internal links
* Traffic
* Page authority
* Future architecture

If an existing URL changes, explicitly require a:

**301 redirect from the old URL to the most relevant replacement URL.**

Never silently change important URLs during the rebuild.

---

## Page Removal and Consolidation

Do not delete an existing page simply because it does not fit the new visual architecture.

Before removing or consolidating a page, evaluate:

* Its purpose
* Search intent
* Organic traffic
* Backlinks
* Internal links
* Unique content
* Conversion value

If pages are consolidated, preserve relevant content and create appropriate redirects.

---

## On-Page SEO Preservation

During migration, intentionally review important pages for:

* URL
* Title tag
* Meta description
* H1
* Heading structure
* Main content
* Search intent
* Internal links
* Images
* Image alt text
* Canonical
* Indexability
* Structured data
* Language relationship

Do not assume rebuilding a page with similar-looking text automatically preserves its SEO value.

---

## Internal Linking

Internal links should support:

* Navigation
* User discovery
* Service relationships
* Location relationships
* SEO
* Conversion

Use descriptive anchors naturally.

Avoid aggressive keyword-stuffed anchor text.

When moving or renaming pages, update internal links rather than relying on redirects indefinitely.

---

## Service Architecture

Service pages should exist when there is meaningful:

* User intent
* Business value
* Content differentiation
* Search opportunity

Do not create dozens of nearly identical service pages purely to target keyword variations.

A service page should provide genuine information relevant to that service.

---

## Location Architecture

The site should support scalable location architecture.

Montreal is currently primary.

Toronto should be supported structurally before launch.

Do not create doorway-style pages consisting of the same copy with city names swapped.

Location pages should contain meaningful location-specific value when eventually published.

---

## Toronto

Prepare the architecture now.

Do not imply Crown Movers operates in Toronto until the business actually does.

When Toronto launches, ensure relevant changes are made across:

* Navigation
* Location pages
* Service/location relationships
* Forms
* Contact information
* Schema
* Internal linking
* English content
* French content where applicable

---

## English and French

Both languages are important.

Polylang Pro will manage language relationships.

The French version should preserve meaning and conversion intent while sounding natural to the intended audience.

Do not mechanically translate English sentence structure when more natural French wording exists.

Maintain equivalent:

* Calls to action
* Forms
* Business facts
* Services
* Important navigation
* SEO intent

where appropriate.

---

## Multilingual SEO

When working on multilingual pages, consider:

* Correct language relationships
* Consistent canonicals
* Indexability
* Localized metadata
* Internal linking
* URL structure
* Sitemap inclusion
* Language switching

Do not automatically canonicalize French pages to English equivalents.

Each legitimate language page should generally be capable of standing as its own localized page.

---

## Page Copy Structure

Copy should generally establish:

1. What the service/page is about
2. Why it matters to the customer
3. How Crown Movers helps
4. Relevant details
5. Trust/evidence
6. What the visitor should do next

Do not rigidly force this sequence when another structure provides better UX.

---

## Headings

Use headings to organize meaning, not merely to make text large.

Each page should normally have one clear H1.

Use H2/H3 hierarchy logically.

Do not choose heading levels based only on visual styling.

Visual appearance belongs to CSS.

Document structure belongs to HTML semantics.

---

## SEO Copy

SEO should not make the copy sound unnatural.

Avoid sentences written solely to insert keywords.

Use relevant terminology where it genuinely helps clarify:

* Service
* Geography
* Customer need
* Move type

Write for humans first while preserving search intent.

---

## Calls to Action

Primary conversion:

**Form submission**

Content should naturally guide appropriate visitors toward requesting a quote.

Do not place identical CTA paragraphs after every short section.

Calls to action should appear at logical decision points.

---

## Reviews and Testimonials

Use genuine customer feedback where available.

Do not rewrite reviews in ways that change the customer's meaning.

Do not invent review excerpts.

Editorial formatting may be cleaned up when it does not alter meaning.

---

## SEO Migration Checklist

Before launch, verify at minimum:

* Existing important URLs accounted for
* Required redirects documented
* Redirects implemented
* No accidental staging indexation
* Production indexability correct
* Canonicals correct
* English/French relationships correct
* Sitemap correct
* Robots directives correct
* Important metadata transferred or intentionally rewritten
* Heading structures reviewed
* Internal links updated
* Broken links checked
* Important images migrated
* Alt text reviewed
* Schema reviewed
* Forms tested
* Analytics/tracking retained or intentionally replaced
* Search Console considerations handled
* 404s reviewed
* Redirect chains avoided

---

## Post-Launch SEO

After launch, the project moves toward active SEO growth.

The rebuild should leave a strong foundation for:

* Local SEO
* Montreal growth
* Toronto growth
* Service expansion
* Location content
* Supporting informational content
* Internal linking
* Schema
* Content optimization
* Conversion optimization

Do not compromise the technical foundation for short-term convenience.

---

## Core SEO Principle

The rebuild should improve the website without needlessly discarding the authority, URLs, content, or search relevance Crown Movers has already accumulated.
